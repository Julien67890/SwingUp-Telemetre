Privacy Policy — SwingUp - Télémètre

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Version française

SwingUp - Télémètre ("the app", "we") is published by Julien Guionie, an independent developer, reachable at developpement24.web@gmail.com. This policy describes what data the app collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

1. Data we collect

1.1 User account (optional)

If you create an account, we collect the following through Firebase Authentication (Google):

  • your email address;
  • your password (never stored in plain text — encrypted and handled by Firebase Authentication);
  • your first name or nickname, if provided;
  • with "Continue with Google": basic profile information provided by Google (name, email, photo).

1.2 Data you create in the app

Once signed in, and during your free trial or subscription, the following is saved in our database (Firestore, hosted by Google) so it can sync across your devices:

  • your bag setup (distance per club);
  • green outlines you trace on the map;
  • course names and custom labels you add.

1.3 Location

The app uses your precise GPS location, with your explicit permission, to calculate distances to the green (center, back, left, right) and elevation change. This location is used on your device for the calculation; it is not stored in our database or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

1.4 Audience measurement

With your consent (banner shown on first visit), we use Google Analytics to measure overall app usage and improve it. This data is never sold. You can decline this tracking from the banner; the app remains fully functional.

1.5 Subscription and payment (upcoming)

Measuring and mapping are free. Saving your greens will be an optional subscription, preceded by a one-month free trial. Payment is not active yet. Once available:

  • in the Android app installed from Google Play, payment is processed by Google Play under its own privacy policy;
  • on the web version, payment is processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

We never receive your banking details: we only keep your subscription status (active, trial, ended) and its end date.

3. Data retention

  • Your account, bag and profile are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Your saved greens are kept during your free trial and subscription. If the trial or subscription ends without renewal, they are kept for 30 days, with a warning in the app, and then permanently deleted.

4. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, and object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact developpement24.web@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

5. Deleting your account

In the app: My account → Delete my account. Your account, greens and bag are deleted immediately and permanently. You can also email developpement24.web@gmail.com from the address linked to your account; requests are processed within 30 days. If you have an active subscription, cancel it from Google Play or your subscription settings before deleting your account.

6. Security

Passwords are encrypted and handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them in plain text. All communication uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Intended audience

SwingUp - Télémètre is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, in particular when payment is activated. Any material change will be noted in the app or on this page, with an updated date at the top.

9. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: developpement24.web@gmail.com.
