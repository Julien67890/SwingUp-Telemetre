Privacy Policy — SwingUp Rangefinder

Last updated: August 27, 2026

SwingUp Rangefinder ("the app", "we") is published by Julien Guionie, an independent developer, reachable at developpement24.web@gmail.com. This policy describes what data the app collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

1. Data we collect

1.1 User account (optional)

If you create an account to save your setup, we collect the following through Firebase Authentication (Google):

  • Your email address
  • Your password (never stored in plain text — handled and encrypted by Firebase Authentication)
  • Your first name / nickname, if provided
  • If you use "Sign in with Google": basic profile information (name, email, photo) provided by Google

1.2 Data you create in the app

Once signed in, the following is saved in our database (Firestore, hosted by Google) so it can sync across your devices:

  • Your bag setup (distance per club)
  • Green outlines you trace on the map
  • Course names and custom labels you add

1.3 Location

The app uses your precise GPS location, with your explicit permission, to calculate distances to the green (center, back, left, right) and elevation change. This location is used locally on your device for the distance calculation and is not stored in our database or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

1.4 Audience measurement

With your consent (banner shown on first visit), we use Google Analytics to measure overall app usage (page views, traffic) and improve it. Usage data is never sold to third parties. You can decline this tracking from the consent banner; the app remains fully functional without it.

1.5 Payments (upcoming feature)

A payment feature via Stripe is in preparation and is not yet active. Once available, payment information (card number, etc.) will be processed directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy — we never store this information on our servers.

3. Data retention

Account and gameplay data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can request full deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see section 5).

4. Your rights (GDPR)

If you live in the European Union or another territory covered by the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, and object to the processing of your personal data. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at developpement24.web@gmail.com.

5. Deleting your account

To delete your account and all associated data, send a request to developpement24.web@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Your request will be processed within 30 days.

6. Security

Passwords are managed and encrypted by Firebase Authentication; we never have access to them in plain text. The app communicates with its servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Intended audience

SwingUp Rangefinder is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children of that age.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, in particular when the Stripe payment feature is activated. Any material change will be noted in the app or on this page, with an updated date at the top of this document.

9. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, write to us at developpement24.web@gmail.com.
